# METALGORE Art Direction

- **Style:** Low-poly PlayStation 2-era industrial brutalism: open city spaces enclosed by the moving megastructure, with restrained botanical and bodily details. The interface uses an original, high-contrast technical layout, informed by broad genre references rather than reproducing another game's screens.
- **Palette:** floor `#303333`; walls `#1C1F1F`; structures `#454A47`; route `#4D7A78`; props `#594F3D`; shadow `#101212`; signal `#B37314`; boss `#851F1A`.
- **Mood:** Hard industrial light, cool ambient shadows, amber navigation cues, and muted red around boss spaces.
